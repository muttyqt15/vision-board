Complete Vision
When the product is done
👩‍🏫
The Teacher
Spends less time on admin than any peer. Lesson plans, quizzes, and rapor comments take minutes. Their job is teaching, relationships, and judgment — not paperwork.
🏫
The Principal
Sees every classroom in real time — attendance, grades, risk flags, teacher activity — without asking anyone for a report. Decisions are data-backed, not intuition-based.
📱
The Student & Parent
Always know where they stand. Not twice a year through a paper rapor — continuously, via WhatsApp. No student is ever surprised by a grade at semester end.
🏛️
The Yayasan
Compares performance across all affiliated schools. Identifies which schools need intervention. Sees the return on their investment in concrete academic outcome trends.
What never
changes
Bahasa Indonesia is first-class
Every phase, every feature, every AI output — written, reviewed, and refined in Indonesian. Never shipped before a native speaker has checked it.
Mobile is the primary platform
Every feature designed for one thumb on a mid-range Android before it exists on desktop. Always tested on a Galaxy A-series.
AI creates, teachers decide
AI suggestions are always visible and reviewable before being applied. We never auto-fill a field the teacher didn't ask to fill.
Free tier is permanent
Individual teachers always have access to a genuinely useful free product. The distribution engine never gets turned off.
Tenant isolation is sacred
No school ever sees another school's data — not by accident, not through a URL, not through our own tooling. Enforced at the database layer first.
No empty states, ever
Every screen a new user sees contains something — suggestions, examples, pre-written templates. The product feels alive from the moment of signup.
